# Copy Review Checklist (NT edition)

Merges Whitman's 46-point "killer ad" checklist, Bly's 4 U's and headline tips, Kennedy's strategic/style/passion rewrites, Hopkins's salesperson test, Caples's tests, Masterson/Forde's Rule of One, Brown's Big Idea criteria. Score each item **Y / N / n.a.**; any **N** in a **Critical** item blocks release.

## A. Strategy (Critical)
- [ ] **Audience** is specific and evidenced (not "everyone")
- [ ] **Awareness** stage identified and the lead matches it
- [ ] **Sophistication** stage identified and the claim/mechanism matches it
- [ ] **One Big Idea / Primary Promise** is clear (30-second test passed)
- [ ] **One emotion**, **one story/angle**, **one action**
- [ ] **Mechanism** is real and explained (or not claimed)
- [ ] **Promise ≤ proof**
- [ ] The **offer** is clear: deliverables, price, terms, risk reversal, reason why
- [ ] The **next step** is explicit and easy

## B. Headline / hook / subject
- [ ] Features the biggest benefit **to the reader** (Whitman #1 rule)
- [ ] Selects the right prospect ("Where's the hernia?")
- [ ] Scores ≥3 on at least three of the 4 U's (Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific, Useful)
- [ ] Specific numbers/names where possible
- [ ] No blind/cute wording; no irrelevant shock
- [ ] Matches the traffic source's promise (message match)
- [ ] Positive framing unless negative is justified and tested
- [ ] Not a lie, not clickbait

## C. First sentence / first screen
- [ ] Flows from the headline and sets up the second sentence
- [ ] Gets into reader benefit rather than company bragging
- [ ] Contains a self-sufficient mini-version of the message (digital)
- [ ] Short; uses "you"
- [ ] Doesn't satisfy curiosity too early (if curiosity-led)

## D. Body copy
- [ ] Logical progression (chosen skeleton) with no detours
- [ ] Benefits ranked; features translated to benefits (means–end chain)
- [ ] Vivid, sensory, concrete language (mental movies) — no abstractions without proof
- [ ] Proof near each major claim (numbers, demos, testimonials, third-party)
- [ ] Objections listed and answered (direct answer + proof + guarantee reminder)
- [ ] Real drawbacks/limits acknowledged where it builds trust
- [ ] Mechanism explained in selling language
- [ ] Not hype: believable, respectful of intelligence
- [ ] Enthusiasm matches the product (not "cold fish", not shouting)
- [ ] Repetition: core promise restated in different forms
- [ ] Subheads tell the story for skimmers; bullets for lists; captions on images
- [ ] Average sentence length ≈ 6–16 words; varied rhythm; simple words
- [ ] Paragraphs short; adequate white space
- [ ] Read aloud with no stumbles

## E. Offer & close
- [ ] Offer stack and price framing honest and understandable
- [ ] Guarantee clear and as strong as the business can honour
- [ ] Bonuses relevant and real
- [ ] Deadline/scarcity real and logged; reason stated
- [ ] CTA specific ("what you do, what happens next, what you get")
- [ ] Ease of action: all necessary contact/payment options; low friction
- [ ] P.S. restates the offer/benefit/deadline
- [ ] Repeat CTA at decision points

## F. Design & format (as applicable)
- [ ] Looks inviting/editorial, not cluttered; strong focal point
- [ ] Headline large and legible; sensible line breaks
- [ ] Readable type (serif for long print; sans for screens); few type styles
- [ ] Relevant imagery with captions; face looking at viewer when natural
- [ ] Mobile layout works; accessible contrast and alt text
- [ ] Tracking in place (keys, UTMs, codes)

## G. Truth, ethics, compliance (Critical)
- [ ] Every factual claim has a source tag and has been verified
- [ ] No invented testimonials, customers, studies, statistics or experts
- [ ] Testimonials: real, consented, typical-results context if required
- [ ] Health/finance/income/legal claims comply with regulations and platform policies
- [ ] Urgency/scarcity genuine; no fake timers
- [ ] Recurring charges, trials and cancellation terms clear
- [ ] Endorsement/affiliate/sponsorship disclosed
- [ ] Privacy/consent requirements met for the channel and jurisdiction
- [ ] AI-generated elements checked by a human; no personal data misuse
- [ ] No manipulation of vulnerable groups; the "newspaper test" passes

## H. Learning
- [ ] A test plan exists (variable, metric, sample, stop rule)
- [ ] Results will be logged

## Scoring
- 90%+ of non-critical items Y and all critical items Y → ship to test.
- Any critical N → fix before release.
- Many N in section D → return to strategy (A) before polishing.
