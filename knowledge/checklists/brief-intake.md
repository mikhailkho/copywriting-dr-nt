# Brief Intake — what the skill needs before it writes

Ask for these in as few messages as possible. Mark each **Have / Need / Unknown**. If critical items are Unknown, say what is missing, produce a *hypothesis draft* clearly labelled as such, and design the first test to learn the missing fact.

## Critical (block final copy if missing)
1. **Goal & action:** what single action should the reader take? How is success measured?
2. **Offer:** what exactly is sold (deliverables, price, terms), plus guarantee/bonus/deadline, and the reason for any deadline.
3. **Audience:** who, with their words; where they are in awareness (and evidence).
4. **Product facts:** how it works (mechanism), differentiators, numbers, limits.
5. **Proof inventory:** results, testimonials with permission, data, third-party validation, guarantees we can honour.
6. **Channel & format:** page/email/ad/script/letter; length and placement; specs.
7. **Compliance context:** jurisdictions, sector rules, disclaimers required.

## Important
8. **Competitors & claims** they make (sophistication evidence); alternatives the buyer uses.
9. **Customer language:** reviews, calls, tickets, forum posts (verbatim).
10. **Objections** and what usually stops purchase.
11. **Brand voice:** personality, words to use/avoid, past copy that performed well/badly.
12. **Funnel context:** where the traffic comes from, what happens next, follow-up sequence.
13. **Constraints:** legal approvals, brand rules, deadlines, budget.

## Useful
14. **Existing control** and its results; past test log.
15. **Seasonality/news hooks** and key dates.
16. **Persona/spokesperson** (real) and permissible stories.
17. **Metrics baseline:** traffic, conversion, AOV, LTV, CAC.
18. **Operational capacity** (fulfilment, support) for any guarantee/promise.

## Intake prompt (to the user)
```
To write this well I need a few things. Paste what you have; say "unknown" for the rest:
1) The ONE action you want and how you'll measure it.
2) The offer (what they get, price/terms, guarantee, bonus, deadline + why).
3) Who it's for + 10–20 real customer quotes (reviews/calls/tickets).
4) How it works (the true mechanism), 3 proof points with numbers, and any limits.
5) Where this runs (channel, jurisdiction) and any claims/legal constraints.
6) Competitors' main claims (links).
7) Past winners/losers and your brand voice (a paragraph you love).
```

## Missing-information protocol
- **Never invent** facts, numbers, quotes, customers, credentials or timelines.
- Use bracketed placeholders: `[NEED: customer quote about setup time]`.
- Offer a **Plan B** if facts are thin: lower-claim copy, an interview script to collect proof, a test to validate demand.

## Output header (include in deliverables)
```
BRIEF SUMMARY: goal / action / metric
AUDIENCE & DIAGNOSIS: awareness / sophistication / desire
BIG IDEA CARD: promise / mechanism / emotion / story / action
OFFER: ...
CLAIMS REGISTER: claim → evidence → status
ASSUMPTIONS & UNKNOWNS: ...
```
