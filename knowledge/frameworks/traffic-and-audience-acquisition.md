# Traffic and Audience Acquisition

Sources: Brunson (*Traffic Secrets*: Dream 100, work in / buy in, owned traffic, retargeting, cold bridge, funnel hub, affiliates; *Lead Funnels*: lead magnet and opt-in patterns), Halbert (market and list first), Kennedy (acquisition economics), Schwartz (awareness), Hopkins (tested, keyed media).

Copy only works if the right people see it. This file is the **planning layer before the copy**: find where the audience already is, choose how to reach them, and keep the attention you earn.

## 1. Order of operations
1. **Who** (one avatar, one core desire; see `research-and-customer-insight.md`).
2. **Where** (the Dream 100 list per platform; see §2).
3. **Temperature** (hot / warm / cold → the bridge length; see `awareness-and-sophistication.md`, `funnels-and-sequences.md`).
4. **Hook, Story, Offer** per ad, post or email (see `headlines-and-leads.md`, `story-and-belief.md`, `offer-construction.md`).
5. **Capture** (lead magnet or break-even first offer).
6. **Follow up** (sequences; retargeting).
7. **Measure** (cost per lead, per customer, payback; see `testing-and-measurement.md`).

## 2. The Dream 100 worksheet
For each platform your buyers use, list people, brands, groups, shows, newsletters, blogs, channels and (search) keywords that already hold them.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name / URL | Verify; never invent |
| Platform / format | Podcast, newsletter, group, channel, search term |
| Audience size / engagement | Evidence or `UNKNOWN` |
| Overlap with our avatar | Why they're congregated here |
| Relationship status | Cold / following / customer / replied / partner |
| Plan | Serve, interview, guest, co-create, buy ad, affiliate |
| Next action + date | |

Rules: start in your **submarket**, not only your exact niche; keep a separate list per platform; refresh two or three times a year; mark who has a reachable owned channel (list, show) versus audience only.

## 3. Earn, buy, own
| Type | How | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Earned** (work in) | Interviews, guest posts, collabs, community help, shout-outs, being a good customer | Trust, free, compounding | Slow, hard to scale |
| **Controlled** (buy in) | Ads to followers/interests/search; sponsored placements; solo email; podcast ads | Fast, testable, scalable | Stops when paid; platform dependent |
| **Owned** | Email list, subscribers, community you host | Repeat sales at no media cost; survives platform changes | Needs consent, deliverability and a relationship |

Strategy: use earned and controlled traffic to build owned. Maintain at least one owned channel per audience.

## 4. Relationship moves (work in)
Learn what they publish; become a real customer; comment usefully; help before asking; send a *personal* message; don't open with your story; don't ask for a favour in the first contact; offer something that fits their audience (a guest, an asset, a case study). Give them early access to products so endorsements are honest. Always disclose paid or affiliate relationships.

## 5. Paid acquisition rules (buy in)
- Many **distinct hooks** beat one perfect ad. Aim for fresh creative on a regular cadence.
- Target order: Dream 100 audiences → avatar interests → overlaps → lookalikes from your best customers.
- **Prospecting** finds people; **retargeting** converts them. Retargeting audiences by depth: engaged (short window) → visited (slightly longer) → owned (lead/customer). Each stage has its own message and a fixed arc written once.
- Give each new funnel a **small test budget**; scale only what meets payback targets; log each test.
- Expect prospecting to look expensive; judge the whole funnel (lead → first purchase → ascension), not the first click.
- Respect platform policies and targeting restrictions; don't rely on a single platform.

## 6. Cold traffic (long bridge)
- Build a cold-traffic avatar: same person earlier in the journey (problem-aware only).
- Speak the problem in their words; avoid your jargon.
- Bridge with an article, story, quiz or short video before the offer.
- Capture an email, then sequence the education and offer over days or weeks.
- Expect slower payback; have the ladder and follow-up in place first.
- Master hot and warm traffic before scaling cold.

## 7. Branded search and the hub
People who see an ad often search your name, brand or product later. Own that landing experience with a simple hub site (who you are, what you stand for, proof, content, first-step offer, next steps). Check what ranks for your branded terms and claim the first page.

## 8. Partner distribution
- Solo email placements: the publisher sends from their own system; judge by clicks per send and list health; never accept a raw list file.
- Integration: arrange to be included in a partner's welcome, post-purchase or nurture sequence.
- Affiliates: provide a launch calendar, swipe copy, training, tracking and generous but sustainable commission; recruit customers first.
- Always label sponsored content and affiliate links.

## 9. Content and "your own show"
- One primary show channel (writing, audio or video) plus email as distribution.
- Publish on a fixed schedule for months before judging; test material in public; document rather than perform expertise.
- Use interviews with Dream 100 members to build relationships and reach.
- Repurpose a weekly master recording into native pieces for each platform; don't paste identical content everywhere.

## 10. Metrics
Cost per click/lead/customer · opt-in rate · lead quality (e.g., booked, qualified) · first-purchase rate · payback days · revenue per lead at 30/60/90 days · retargeting lift · affiliate EPC · list growth and complaint/unsubscribe rate · branded search volume (a leading indicator).

## 11. Compliance and ethics
- Email/SMS: consent, easy opt-out, honest sender and subject lines; don't buy or scrape lists (Singapore: PDPA, Spam Control Act; elsewhere: CAN-SPAM, GDPR/ePrivacy, etc.).
- Ads: platform policy; substantiated claims; clear disclosure for endorsements and affiliate offers.
- Scarcity and urgency: real only.
- Targeting: avoid inferring or exploiting sensitive attributes (health, finances, relationships) in ways that mislead or harm.
- Data: collect only fields you need; store securely.

## 12. Failure modes
- Only one platform; no owned list.
- Ads without a break-even or payback plan.
- One creative, tested once.
- Skipping the cold bridge and blaming the traffic.
- Pitching in communities, then wondering why you were banned.
- Copying a competitor's page and calling it modelling.
- Hype claims that the platform or regulator will remove.
