# Channel: Letters, Direct Mail & Physical Pieces (and their digital echoes)

Sources: Halbert (Boron Letters Ch. 11–19), Kennedy (Ultimate Sales Letter), Hopkins Ch. 19 (letters), Bly Ch. 7, Caples Ch. 13.

Physical mail is a small share of marketing now, but it remains one of the best **teachers** of principles and is still used for high-value B2B, local business, fundraising, financial/membership and reactivation. Digital echoes are listed.

## 1. The package (classic) and its digital translation
| Classic element | Principle | Digital echo |
|---|---|---|
| **Outer envelope** that looks personal (live stamp, hand/typed address, return address only) | A-pile vs B-pile; get opened | Sender name, subject line, preheader, plain-text style email |
| **Relevant attention-grabber** attached to the letter (sand, peso, dollar bill) | Tie the gimmick to the message | Pattern-interrupting but relevant creative, unexpected first line, prop in video |
| **Date/time at top, personal salutation, "where I am as I write"** | Immediacy and intimacy | Specific timestamps ("Tuesday, 5:41 p.m."), personal opener, context line |
| **(over)** / "go to page 7" guidance | Lead the reader by the hand | Scroll cues, "keep reading" bridges, jump links |
| **Hidden order card** revealed after the letter | Don't pitch too early | Show price/CTA after value builds (but don't hide terms) |
| **Reply envelope options** (stamped, BRE, PSH) | Reduce friction vs. cost; personal vs. commercial | Reply-to-email, one-click forms, prefilled links |
| **Guilt reply** ("if you can't, tell me so I can offer it to someone else") | Commitment; reciprocity | "Reply 'no' and I'll stop emailing" (honest) |
| **P.S.** | Second headline | Same |
| **Teaser at the bottom of each page** | Keep reading | Open loops; "next section" teasers |
| **Handwritten margins, highlighter, simulated notes** | Personal feel, emphasis | Annotated screenshots, hand-drawn elements |
| **Snapshot of the product (not a catalog)** | Do the sell without looking like a sale | Single-product personal email with an image |
| **Sequence of 3 letters** | Repetition; dunning-letter cadence | 3–5 email/SMS touches |

## 2. Letter structure
1. Personal salutation (or segment flag)
2. Extended-headline first paragraph
3. Story/lead per diagnosis
4. Benefits & mechanism
5. Proof (specific)
6. Objections handled
7. Offer with reason why
8. Close with instructions and deadline
9. Signature (real person, name, title)
10. P.S. (restate + incentive)
11. Optional P.P.S.

## 3. Length, layout, type (Halbert/Kennedy)
Long copy for qualified recipients; wide margins; short paragraphs; serif for print readability; sparing underline/bold/second color (red); readable photos; indent paragraphs; avoid ornate letterhead; keep consistent type styles; use subheads and bullet lists for skimmers; teasers at page ends.

## 4. Unit economics (Halbert)
Compute profit per thousand before mailing; staged rollout (1–5k test → 20–100k → rollout). Track keyed response, cost per order, refund rate. Use list hierarchy (own customers > recent multi-buyers of similar products > high unit-of-sale > recency).

## 5. Dated mechanics (don't copy literally)
Postage meters, bulk-rate indicia, SRDS list books, BRE permits. Replace with current postal rules, compiled-list vendors with compliant data, and variable-data printing.

## 6. Compliance
Data protection and marketing consent laws for postal marketing vary (e.g., PDPA/GDPR-type rules for personal data and suppression lists). Fundraising has special rules. Prize/sweepstake mailings are heavily regulated in many countries. Check before sending.
