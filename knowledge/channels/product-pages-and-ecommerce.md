# Channel: Product Pages & Ecommerce

Sources: Hopkins (full story, samples, names), Whitman (value, buying ease, guarantees), Bly (catalog copy; Blue Nile critique), Kennedy (testimonials, photos, prove ease of use), Caples (be specific, free information), Halbert (believability via exact detail), Schwartz (physical vs functional product).

## 1. Principle: sell the functional product
Customers buy what the product *does* (Schwartz). Specs and materials are evidence of the benefit, not the headline. Convert each feature to a benefit and a proof.

## 2. Product page anatomy
1. **Title:** product name + the key benefit/differentiator.
2. **Hero image/video:** show it in action; scale, context; multiple angles; real use.
3. **Price & offer:** price clarity; shipping, returns, delivery date; payment plans where relevant.
4. **Benefit bullets (3–6):** result-first, specific, scannable.
5. **Short story paragraph:** who it's for, what problem it solves, how it works (mechanism).
6. **Proof:** reviews with specifics, ratings count, UGC photos, press, certifications, lab tests.
7. **Details:** specs, materials, sizing, compatibility, care, FAQs.
8. **Risk reversal:** guarantee, returns, warranty in plain words.
9. **Cross-sell/bundle:** "complete the set" with real logic.
10. **Reassurance:** delivery, security, support contact.

## 3. Copy rules
- **Specific over generic:** "Holds 42 oz, keeps cold 24 hours (tested at 35 °C)" vs "keeps drinks cold".
- **Name the use case and the user:** "for light sleepers in apartments".
- **Answer the silent questions:** Will it fit? How long to arrive? What if I don't like it?
- **Sensory language:** texture, sound, taste, fit; mental movies.
- **Comparison table** vs alternatives (honest).
- **Honest drawbacks:** who it's not for (builds trust, lowers returns).
- **Shipping/returns upfront.**

## 4. Collections & category pages
Short intro with the category's key decision criteria; filters by buyer needs; featured "best for…" picks; links to guides.

## 5. Reviews & UGC
Encourage specifics ("What were you using before?"); show distribution including mixed reviews; respond to criticism; moderate fake reviews; disclose incentives; don't write reviews yourself.

## 6. Cart & checkout microcopy
Reduce friction; guest checkout; delivery estimate; trust badges (real); error messages that help; clear total cost; post-purchase email that sets expectations.

## 7. Email & retargeting for ecommerce
Welcome series (brand story + best sellers + first-order incentive if sustainable); browse/cart abandonment (help, not guilt); post-purchase (care tips, accessories, review request); replenishment; win-back.

## 8. Testing priorities for product pages
Offer/shipping/returns > hero image/video > benefit bullets > price display/payment plan > proof placement > page length > CTA wording.

## 9. Compliance
Accurate product claims; "Made in" and sustainability claims substantiated; reference prices real; free shipping thresholds clear; subscription disclosures; review integrity.

## 10. AI notes
Use Claude to: rewrite spec lists into benefit/proof pairs; create FAQ from support tickets; generate comparison tables from provided data; A/B title variants. Do **not** have it invent technical specs, certifications or reviews.
