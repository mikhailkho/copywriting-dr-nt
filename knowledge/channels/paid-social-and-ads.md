# Channel: Paid Social, Search & Short-Form Video Ads

Sources: Hopkins (headlines, keyed ads, small-space discipline), Caples (headline formulas, small ads), Schwartz (awareness-matched headlines), Brown (Big Idea lifts CTR and lowers cost; mental opt-out), Whitman (22 starters; response superchargers), Halbert (relevant attention, first impression), Bly (4 U's).

For audience targeting order, prospecting vs retargeting, creative volume and payback rules see `../frameworks/traffic-and-audience-acquisition.md`.

## 1. Ad job
**Stop the right person and move them to the next step** (click, watch, register). The ad is a headline plus a micro-lead; the landing page continues the same idea (message match).

## 2. Match ad type to awareness
| Audience | Ad approach | Example frames |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold / unaware** | Story, curiosity, identification, news-like insight | "Why ___ people in ___ are doing ___" (only if true), founder insight, myth-busting |
| **Problem-aware** | Name the problem in their words + hint of mechanism | "If ___ keeps happening, here's why" |
| **Solution-aware** | Promise + mechanism; comparison | "How ___ works differently from ___" |
| **Product-aware / retargeting** | Proof, objection-handling, risk reversal, case results | Review snippets, demo clips, FAQ cards |
| **Most aware** | Offer, deadline, bonus | "Ends Friday: ___" (real) |

## 3. Anatomy
- **Hook (first line / first 1–3 seconds):** selects audience + Big Idea. Use formulas as variant generators (Caples 35; Whitman 22; Kennedy fill-ins).
- **Body:** 2–5 short lines or 15–45 seconds: promise → mechanism → proof → CTA.
- **Creative:** reinforces the hook; human face; relevant demonstration; captions on video; strong first frame; avoid stock-photo sameness.
- **CTA:** one action; benefit-oriented button text.
- **Landing page:** same promise; fast; mobile-friendly.

## 4. Short-form video script skeleton (20–45 s)
1. Hook (flag audience + curiosity/benefit)
2. Problem/desire in one sentence
3. Mechanism or demonstration
4. Proof snippet (number, quote, before/after if truthful)
5. Offer/CTA
Variants: founder talk-to-camera, customer testimonial, UGC-style review (disclosed), screen-record demo, myth-vs-fact.

## 5. Search ads
- Mirror the query's language; include the specific benefit and a differentiator; qualify (price range, location, "for ___") to repel poor clicks; use extensions for proof and offer; align landing page headline to keyword intent.
- Negative keywords and match types are part of "selecting the right prospects" (headline principle).

## 6. Social carousel / image ads
Slide 1 = hook; slides 2–4 = proof/mechanism; last slide = CTA. Text hierarchy; single idea per ad set.

## 7. Variation strategy
1. Test **3–5 distinct ideas/angles** (not wording tweaks).
2. For the winning idea, test **lead formats** (question / story / proof / offer).
3. Then test creative formats and CTAs.
Keep the offer and landing page constant during creative tests.

## 8. Policy & compliance
Platform policies restrict certain claims (health, finance, employment, housing, before/after images, personal attributes, misleading urgency). Review current policy text. Use substantiated claims, clear disclosures, and avoid targeting that implies sensitive attributes. Ads for regulated sectors may require pre-approval. Endorsements and influencer content need clear disclosure of material connections.

## 9. Metrics
Hook rate (3-second views), hold rate, CTR, cost per qualified lead/purchase, landing conversion, revenue per click, creative fatigue (frequency), comment sentiment. Judge winners by down-funnel economics.

## 10. AI notes
- Generate hooks across lead archetypes; label each; score with 4 U's.
- Make a "claim register" for each ad listing claims and proof.
- Beware sameness across a batch; require different underlying ideas, not just different words.
- Keep a human in the loop on testimonials and before/after content.
